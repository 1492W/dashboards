B) Revenue Logic & Growth Decomposition

Ad & Marketing revenues dominate (86% of total) via CPM/CPC in feed, display, and search channels. VAS (14%) includes memberships and game services. Growth drivers: AI-driven targeting improvements, feed pivot engagement uplift, and emerging verticals in local services.

Revenue Structure

Ad & Marketing: ~86% of revenue ($1.50B FY2025). Monetized via CPM/CPC in feed ads, social display, topic/search ads. Content marketing ~50% of ad revenue.

VAS: ~14% ($256M FY2025). Memberships, game-related services.

Growth Decomposition:

  • Volume: MAU declining but feed pivot aims to boost engagement per user
  • Price: AI improving CPM through better targeting; 40% of creatives AI-generated
  • Mix: Content marketing stable at 50%; performance-based RTB ads growing fast
  • New: Local services and e-commerce emerging as growth verticals

C) Margin Drivers & Cost Evolution

Gross margin stable near 79%. Operating margin compressed to 21% (Non-GAAP) in Q4 FY2025 from strategic reinvestment in AI and feed pivot. S&M largest swing factor; R&D cut 26% over 2 years. Cash conversion strong: ~$478M FCF in FY2025 despite margin compression.

Gross Margin (~79%)

  • Cost of revenues declining ($401M→$370M)
  • Bandwidth/server costs optimized
  • Near-zero marginal cost for ad inventory
  • Sustainable at 78-80% range

Operating Margin

  • GAAP: 28% FY2024 peak
  • Non-GAAP: 30% historical
  • S&M largest swing factor
  • R&D cut 26% over 2 years
  • SBC declined 38% ($112M→$70M)
  • Q4 dropped to 21% from strategic investment

Cash Conversion

  • FCF ~$478M FY2025
  • Capex only $42M (asset-light)
  • Strong working capital (prepaid advertisers)
  • Cash position $2.41B ($10/share)
  • High conversion despite margin squeeze
