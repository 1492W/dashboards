Key Takeaways — Q4 2025

Maturity Phase Dominance

Mature centers now generate 57% of total center revenue (¥142.8M of ¥249M), up from just 17% in Q4 2024. As more centers cross the 12-month mark, revenue quality improves structurally.

Avg Rev/Center Recovering

Blended avg revenue per center rose to ¥5.1M from ¥4.7M in Q3. Mature centers at ¥8.4M show the target unit economics — the gap between mature and growth-phase (¥4.7M) defines the improvement runway.

Ramp-up Drag Manageable

13 ramp-up centers contribute only 7% of revenue (¥16.9M) but represent 27% of center count. As these mature over 2–3 quarters, they'll shift from drag to contribution — but 35+ new openings in 2026 will reload this bucket.
