i The Three Commercial Model Words Explained

套餐化
Package-ization / Bundling

Customer buys complete solutions, not individual products. Designer (via M+) curates 整案设计 (whole-case design) bundling furniture + electronics + renovation into one ticket.

Example: Master bedroom package = bed + nightstand + smart lock + lighting + curtain + paint + AC + bedding = ¥80,000 sold as one
⚠ Tension: Zhu Jiagui explicitly rejected "套餐 mode" for design service ("design is not bulk discount"). Applies to product bundling at brand level, NOT standardized design packages.

场景化
Scenario-ization

Display products in full room contexts, not booth-style. Customer experiences "what life would be like" rather than imagining alone.

Example: "Modern Scandinavian living room" displayed as complete room with sofa + lighting + decor + smart TV + speakers + plants integrated
Inspiration: IKEA showrooms · MUJI Hotel · Nitori scenario displays. Currently concentrated on top floor of flagship malls; ground floors still booth-style.

家居生活百货化
Home-Life Dept-Store-ization

Macalline becomes "department store of home" — wider product mix beyond furniture. New benchmark: 华润万象城/龙湖/万达综合体 (shopping mall complexes).

Honest assessment — top-floor experiment reality:

Based on on-the-ground observation: M+ Design Centers occupy the top floor of each flagship mall (10–15% of GFA) with scenario displays and designer studios. Ground floors still operate the legacy booth-rental model. Underperforming malls in tier-3 cities (e.g., Kunming Chenggong: 50%+ vacancy observed Jan 2026) have not been converted to the new model — they are candidates for closure (42 malls closed in 2025).

The strategic logic implied by "30+10+1": Macalline is explicitly accepting that the new model cannot scale across all 292 malls. The bifurcation thesis stands: ~30 premium malls become "Macalline 2.0" (designer-led, scenario-based, REIT-eligible) · the remaining ~260 stay on the legacy model or close. Total mall count likely contracts from 292 → 250-270 by end-2026.

Capital implication: Vice GM Zhu Jiagui acknowledged "数十亿" (tens of billions yuan) invested in mall infrastructure upgrade and a 3-5 year incubation period before M+ generates full economics. Combined with the "533" designer subsidy (50% rent discount, 3-year lease, 3-month rent-free), Macalline is forgoing roughly ¥175-200M annual rent on M+ space to seed the ecosystem. Capex required for 30 flagship transformation ≈ ¥300-900M total over 3-5 years, against FY2025 OCF of ¥816M. Implies persistent negative free cash flow in the transition period.
