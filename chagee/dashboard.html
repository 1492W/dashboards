What looks strong

  • Footprint growth: moving from a debut store into a broader SoCal cluster is a meaningful signal.
  • Marketing energy: launches are getting attention in local press and lifestyle channels.
  • Digital stack: rewards + ordering + fresh app updates imply retention is a priority.
  • Corporate support: overseas growth still looks strong enough to justify continued expansion.

What still needs proof

  • Repeat behavior: public data cannot show 30/60/90-day repeat rate.
  • Unit economics: no public read on four-wall margin, labor pressure, or rent efficiency.
  • Same-store stability: no clean public proof that demand holds after novelty fades.
  • Product love: taste/value perception is not yet decisively strong across public commentary.
