Executive Snapshot

MONITORING
Upgraded from WEAKENING

Conviction Change

Slightly positive. Q4 same-store decline improved to -25.5% from -27.8% in Q3 — first sequential improvement in 5 quarters. Organizational restructuring complete. However, Non-GAAP NI collapsed 84% QoQ to RMB 100M, and management explicitly stated they will prioritize market share over profitability in 2026.

Biggest Changes This Quarter

  • Same-store decline improved sequentially for first time in 5 quarters (-25.5% vs -27.8% Q3)
  • Business model transition to GMV-based revenue sharing with franchisees fully launched from 2026
  • Management explicitly prioritized market share over profitability
  • Active member definition redefined — 44.7M (new) vs 35.2M (old definition)
  • CEO admitted "underestimated the complexity" of managing 3,000+ employee organization
Worst of same-store decline may be behind, but management's decision to prioritize market share over near-term profits means 2026 will be a transition year — long-term compounding depends on whether the franchise model pivot and overseas expansion can rebuild unit economics.

Monitor Next Quarter

  • Q1 2026 same-store sales (must beat -25.5% to confirm trajectory)
  • G&A normalization from 20% toward 13-15% post-restructuring
  • Overseas teahouse unit economics (management claims overseas GMV/store exceeds domestic)

Strategic / Moat Evolution

Pricing Power Weakening
Delivery subsidies created RMB 3-5 price gap vs offline. CEO admitted "underinvested" in offline experience. Won't cut prices but losing volume. Need offline experience investment to justify price gap.
Competitive Position Stable
#1 premium tea retained. "Returning to Yunnan" product achieved 51% dormant member reactivation and 50.2% WoW GMV uplift. Hello Kitty collab resonating in SEA (75% new product share on launch day in Thailand).
Switching Costs Stable
Tea drinks have inherently low switching costs. 44.7M active members (new definition) but engagement quality unclear with 3rd-party platform inclusion.
Operating Leverage Negative
Revenue declining while cost base expanded (615 company-owned stores, overseas infrastructure). G&A jumped to 20% of revenue. Negative leverage as revenue per store declines.
Capital Intensity Increasing
Company-owned grew 169→615 (+264%). US expansion is "decades-long" with high CapEx. Capital-light franchise being supplemented by capital-heavy owned stores.
Moat Durability Monitoring
Brand survived delivery war without price cuts. Product innovation still drives engagement. But >90% revenue from single category (milk tea) and domestic market under structural pressure.
Over 5-10 years, franchise model pivot and overseas expansion could create more durable business if execution succeeds. Key question: does GMV revenue sharing genuinely align incentives, or merely delay franchisee profitability issues? 345 stores across 7 countries (Korea Q2 2026) builds optionality but requires proving product-market fit independently in each market.

  1. Q1 2026 same-store sales — must beat -25.5% to confirm recovery trajectory
  2. G&A as % of revenue — should drop from 20% toward 13-15% post-restructuring
  3. Overseas unit economics disclosure — GMV/store vs domestic, store-level profitability
  4. Franchise revenue per store under new GMV-sharing model
  5. Active member engagement under both old and new definitions
Key Risks Building
  1. Market share vs profitability trade-off could compress 2026 margins beyond guidance
  2. SBC (RMB 723.5M) exceeds Q4 annualized GAAP net income — dilution concern
  3. 8-market overseas expansion may stretch management bandwidth
  4. New franchise revenue-sharing model is untested at scale
Confirmation Signals
  1. Same-store growth turns positive in any quarter of 2026
  2. G&A normalizes below 15% of revenue by Q2 2026
  3. Overseas stores demonstrate profitable unit economics
  4. New product launches maintain >30% dormant member reactivation rate

  1. Same-store decline worsens in Q1 2026 — would invalidate "stabilize H1" guidance
  2. G&A remains above 18% in Q1 2026 — would signal restructuring costs are not one-time
  3. Operating cash flow turns negative in any quarter
  4. Franchise store closures accelerate under new revenue-sharing model
